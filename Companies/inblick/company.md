# Inblick (inblick.ai)

- **Website:** https://inblick.ai
- **Location:** Gothenburg, Sweden
- **What they build:** AI-powered business intelligence platform, natural-language queries that auto-generate insights, targeting schools, municipalities (e.g. Mölndals Stad), and private companies who don't have dedicated data analysts. Version 1.1 shipped GPT-5 and Grok AI-powered insights, PhenixID login, stronger admin controls, and Slack-delivered updates. (Confirmed — Andreas Kaltenbach's own LinkedIn post, 2026)
- **Company structure:** Inblick and Städkollen (a separate AI product for student-housing cleaning quality) are both built under the "Nestic" / "Future Memories" umbrella, a Gothenburg product studio that pivoted from client project work to focus fully on its own AI products. (Confirmed via team LinkedIn profiles)

(Confirmed via LinkedIn)
