## ⚠️ Critical Flag: ITAR / Export-Control Exposure
The Full Stack (Security and Cloud) posting explicitly lists **ITAR-sensitive systems** and "enterprise security reviews" as a plus, and the company's core customer base is aerospace/defense (Anduril, Relativity Space, Hermeus, Mach Industries, NASA). **ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) generally restricts access to controlled technical data to U.S. persons** (citizens/permanent residents) — a Pakistan-based contractor would very likely be legally excluded from any ITAR-touching work, regardless of remote-friendliness in general. This is a **Confirmed legal constraint**, not a soft geographic preference, and is the single biggest risk to this being a viable remote target. I have not found evidence of a non-ITAR engineering track at Vendra — the outreach below is deliberately framed around general full-stack/backend capability rather than the security/cloud req, so Anish can self-select whether any non-restricted work exists.
