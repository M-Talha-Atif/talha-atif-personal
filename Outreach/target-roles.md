## What Makes a Role a Strong Fit (Signal Checklist)

When evaluating a specific job posting, check for these signals:

- **Ownership language:** "own features end to end," "no separate frontend/backend team," "make the calls" — these indicate real ownership, not ticket execution.
- **Stack match:** Java/Spring, Node.js, Python, or a typed/JVM-adjacent language that transfers cleanly (e.g. Kotlin).
- **System complexity:** distributed systems, event-driven architecture, integrations, or compliance/reliability requirements — these are where the real story bank (career.md) applies directly.
- **Small team size:** explicit mention of team size under ~30, or "founding," "early," "small elite team."
- **AI-forward but not AI-only:** companies that mention using AI coding tools as part of the workflow (Claude, Copilot, Cursor) signal a modern, high-leverage engineering culture — a strong positive signal, not a requirement to fake expertise in AI/ML research.

---

## Roles to Decline Even If Offered

- Any role with a job title that says "engineer" but the actual scope is QA, manual testing, or pure support/maintenance with zero feature ownership.
- Any role based in India or Turkey (see ideal-companies.md geographic constraint).
- Any role where the interview process itself signals low engineering maturity (no technical interview at all, or a technical interview that is trivia rather than real problem-solving).
